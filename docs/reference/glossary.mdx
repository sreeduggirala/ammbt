# Glossary

## A

**AMM (Automated Market Maker)**
A decentralized exchange protocol that uses mathematical formulas to price assets instead of order books.

**Active Bin**
In DLMM, the bin containing the current price where trades occur.

## B

**Bin**
A discrete price level in DLMM where liquidity can be placed.

**Bin Step**
The percentage price difference between adjacent bins in DLMM.

## C

**CLMM (Concentrated Liquidity Market Maker)**
AMM design where LPs can concentrate liquidity in specific price ranges (e.g., Uniswap V3).

**CPAMM (Constant Product AMM)**
AMM using x*y=k formula with liquidity spread across all prices (e.g., Uniswap V2).

**Concentrated Liquidity**
Providing liquidity only within a specific price range for higher capital efficiency.

## D

**DLMM (Dynamic Liquidity Market Maker)**
Meteora's AMM design with discrete bins and dynamic fees.

**Drawdown**
The decline from peak to trough in portfolio value.

## F

**Fee Growth**
Accumulated trading fees per unit of liquidity, used for fee calculation in V3.

**Fee Tier**
The swap fee percentage in V3 (0.05%, 0.30%, or 1.00%).

## I

**Impermanent Loss (IL)**
The difference in value between holding tokens in an LP position vs. holding them directly.

**In Range**
A V3/DLMM position where the current price is within the specified bounds.

## L

**Liquidity**
Capital deposited in an AMM pool that enables trading.

**Liquidity Provider (LP)**
Someone who deposits tokens into an AMM pool to earn trading fees.

**LP Tokens**
Tokens representing share of liquidity in a pool (V2).

## M

**Max Drawdown**
The largest peak-to-trough decline during the backtest period.

## N

**Numba**
JIT compiler used by ammBT for performance optimization.

## O

**Out of Range**
A V3/DLMM position where the current price is outside the specified bounds (not earning fees).

## P

**Position**
An LP's stake in a pool, defined by amount and range (V3/DLMM).

**Price**
The exchange rate between two tokens (typically token1/token0).

## R

**Rebalancing**
Adjusting an LP position, typically to recenter the range around current price.

**Reserves**
The amounts of each token held in a pool.

## S

**Sharpe Ratio**
Risk-adjusted return metric: (return - risk-free rate) / volatility.

**Slippage**
The difference between expected and executed trade price.

**Swap**
An exchange of one token for another through the AMM.

## T

**Tick**
A discrete price point in V3, representing 0.01% price change.

**Tick Spacing**
The minimum distance between usable ticks, determined by fee tier.

## V

**Vectorized**
Processing multiple data points simultaneously using array operations.

**Volatility**
The degree of price variation over time.
